## Responsibility and scope

The supplied architecture conversation identifies D0112 Portfolio Risk Engine and D0113 Portfolio Construction. Detailed original D0112 requirements were not recovered. This implementation is the new limited contract in D0112_IMPLEMENTATION_SPEC.md; it does not adopt old estimated percentages/dates or certify full historical intent.

Public API: lib.portfolio_risk.make_request and evaluate_portfolio_risk. Inputs are immutable portfolio positions carrying existing D0111 ReturnComparison records, explicit capital weights, cash, company sector evidence, common currency/cutoff/exit, policy and joint-scenario attestation bound to every constituent hash (including multiplicity). Same company holdings aggregate; inconsistent classification for that company fails.

Weights represent initial capital including entry costs, matching D0111 net-return denominators. Cash return0 is an explicit assumption, not a guarantee or interest estimate. No borrowing, shorts, FX conversion, reinvestment, rebalancing, double cost deduction or automatic allocation.

Per joint scenario, each side sums weight times constituent net return. Delta is replay minus baseline. Bear downside=max(0,-aggregate bear return) AFTER netting constituents; not a sum of separate loss magnitudes. Values are conditional, pretax, nonannualized and unweighted by probability; they are not VaR, maximum loss or a safety assessment.

Capital conservation and company/sector/cash limit comparisons use exact rational Decimal values. Equality to maximum/minimum is permitted; a1e-75 excess is still detected. Decimal displays/calculations use fixed precision50; limits never depend on a rounded display. Results retain full request, inputs, references, rule version, breaches and hash.

## Limits and remaining roadmap

Only declared company/sector/cash constraints are checked. Theme, event, liquidity, correlation, VaR/CVaR and calibrated stress checks are explicitly UNSUPPORTED even if declared_constraints_pass=true. Source hashes/attestation verify internal binding, not authenticity of economic inputs, same-scenario joint realism or source valuation arithmetic. No statistical forecast/portfolio efficacy is accepted.

D0112 v1 minimum is a conditional aggregation/checker, not the whole risk-engine roadmap. Actual-company input quality, portfolio policy suitability and empirical validation remain open. D0113 construction can be designed separately using explicit policy; no optimizer, stock selection, recommendation or real trades are included here. The older G1..G5 scope covers the single-company path, not the expanded portfolio roadmap; do not turn counts or old weights into a new whole-product percentage. Overall percentage remains unassessed.

No merge/FROZEN, scheduler/runtime/source changes or repeated SMM source investigation. Existing frozen and production modules unchanged.
