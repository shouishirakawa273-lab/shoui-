## Delivered

`lib.scenario_returns.compare_scenario_returns` compares baseline/replay valuation outputs using one explicit price, exit mapping, share-basis attestation and dividend/cost assumptions. Company, currency, target period, model, multiple, balance sheet and cutoff must match. Availability after cutoff, invalid hashes, missing scenarios, unconfirmed basis and changes outside the declared macro scenarios are rejected.

For value V, price P, dividend D, entry cost Ce and exit cost Cx:
- gap = (V-P)/P
- gross = (V+D-P)/P
- net = (V+D-Cx-P-Ce)/(P+Ce)
- downside = max(0, -bear net), not maximum loss or VaR
- deltas = replay minus baseline

Ratios are pretax and nonannualized, with no probability weighting. Explicit Decimal context and hashed inputs/results preserve reproducibility. Labels identify observational association, caller-attested basis, and source arithmetic not independently revalidated.
